<p>I am a Research Fellow at the <a href="https://www.lai.fu-berlin.de/en/index.html">Lateinamerika-Institut, Freie Universität Berlin</a>. I am a historian specialising in the 20th-century Cold War in the Caribbean Basin, with a focus on authoritarian internationalism, regional diplomacy, and political violence from a global perspective.</p>

<p>My current research examines the diplomatic and security networks of the early Cold War in the Greater Caribbean Basin (1944–1955). My current manuscript, <em>The Caribbean Basin and the shaping of Latin America’s early Cold War (1944–1955)</em>, is under final review with the University of New Mexico Press. My articles have appeared in leading journals such as <em>Hispanic American Historical Review</em>, <em>The Americas</em>, <em>Latin American Research Review</em>, and the <em>Journal of Latin American Studies</em>.</p> 
  
<p>I'm author of four books, including <em>Manuel Colom Argueta and the Democratic Collapse of the Central American Cold War, 1954–1979</em> by Bloomsbury Academics (Feb. 2026).</p>

<p>In 2026 I was awarded a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowship (European Commission, Seal of Excellence, score 91.8%).</p>

<p>I have held research fellowships at the <a href="https://www.cedla.uva.nl/">CEDLA, University of Amsterdam</a>, the <a href="https://llilas.utexas.edu/">LLILAS-Benson Latin American Studies and Collections, University of Texas at Austin</a>, and a Visiting Fellowship at the <a href="https://ahc.leeds.ac.uk/history">School of History, University of Leeds</a>, where I also taught Latin American History at undergraduate level. I previously served as Head of the J. Joaquín Pardo Chair on Historical Research at the Universidad del Valle de Guatemala.</p>

<p>I hold a Ph.D. in History from <a href="https://www.ciesas.edu.mx/">CIESAS</a> (México, 2020), passed with distinction.</p>
